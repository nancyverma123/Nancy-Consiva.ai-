Q: What are the password requirements when creating an account?
A: Your password must be at least 8 characters long, and include at least one uppercase letter (A-Z), one lowercase letter (a-z), one number (0-9), and one special character (such as @, #, $, or !). The signup form checks these requirements live as you type and shows a green check next to each one once it's satisfied.

---

Q: What information do I need to create an account?
A: You need your First Name, Last Name, Organization Name, an email address (which you verify with a 6-digit code), and a password meeting the strength requirements above. No credit card is required.

---

Q: How do I verify my email during signup?
A: Click "Verify" next to the email field on the signup form. A 6-digit code is sent to that email within seconds. Enter the code — once verified, a green "Verified" badge appears inline next to the email field. You cannot click "Create Account" until this is done.

---

Q: What is the HTML Meta Tag verification method?
A: HTML Meta Tag is one of three domain ownership verification methods (alongside DNS TXT Record and File Upload). Consiva gives you a specific <meta> tag to paste into the <head> section of your site's homepage. Once it's live, click "Check Now" in the Verify Domain dialog to confirm.

---

Q: What is the File Upload verification method?
A: File Upload is one of three domain ownership verification methods. Consiva provides a verification file for you to upload to the root directory of your website (e.g. yoursite.com/verification-file.txt). Once it's accessible at that URL, click "Check Now" to confirm ownership.

---

Q: What is the DNS TXT Record verification method?
A: DNS TXT Record is the default and most common domain verification method. Consiva gives you a Host and a Value to add as a TXT record in your domain's DNS settings. This doesn't require touching any website files — useful if your developer manages DNS but not the codebase. DNS changes can take a few minutes up to 24-48 hours to propagate before the "Check Now" check succeeds.

---

Q: What are the cookie banner layout options?
A: Four layout options are available under Banner Builder → Layout & Style: Bottom Banner, Top Banner, Modal, and Floating Widget.

---

Q: What does the Free plan include?
A: The Free plan includes: 1 domain, 1,000 cookie consents per month, 50 form consents per month, a DPDP-compliant banner in English and Hindi, basic cookie scanning, 30-day analytics, and 1 onboarding session. No credit card is required.

---

Q: What does the Pro plan include?
A: The Pro plan (₹5,999/month) includes: 5 domains, 15,000 cookie consents per month, 1,500 form consents per month, automated/scheduled cookie scanning, DPDP + GDPR banners, all 22 Eighth Schedule languages, enhanced analytics with CSV export, and the CERT-In breach tracker.

---

Q: How do I enable Two-Factor Authentication?
A: Go to Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication, then click "Enable Two-Factor Authentication." This requires an authenticator-app code in addition to your password at every future sign-in.

---

Q: How do I raise a support ticket?
A: Click "Help & Support → New Ticket" from any page in the dashboard. Fill in a Subject, Description, Priority, and Category (and optionally attach screenshots/logs). Response time depends on the priority you select, and you can track the ticket's status from the Support page.
